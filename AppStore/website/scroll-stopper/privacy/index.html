DUCK ON DUTY

Your phone. Your business.

Duck on Duty does not collect personal data, track you, sell data, show ads, or use third-party analytics. There is no account.

What stays on your iPhone

Apple’s Screen Time framework supplies opaque identifiers for the apps you choose. The app and its extensions share these identifiers, your limits, break deadlines, recent interruption times and language preference locally so that the reminders work. Duck on Duty does not read what you view, type or send.

Your controls

You can change your selection in Your limits, pause new breaks in settings, or revoke Screen Time authorization in iOS Settings. Deleting Duck on Duty removes its local app data.

Contact

If you email support, your message and email address are used to reply to you. Support email is optional and separate from the app.
