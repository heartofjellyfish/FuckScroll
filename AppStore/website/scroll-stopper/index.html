DUCK ON DUTY

Talk to the duck.

Need help? Email support. Include your iOS version and what happened. No need to share your app list.

Set a limit

Allow Screen Time, choose individual apps and set a usage limit and break for each. Nearby visits add up. When usage records show you stayed away for the length of the break, your timer starts fresh. You can close Duck on Duty after saving.

Still blocked?

Open Duck on Duty to refresh a finished break. When several apps are resting, each icon has its own countdown. Turning the duck off stops new breaks; current breaks still finish.

Make it sound like you

Ground rules → Edit break screen lets you write the reminder and exit-button text. Try in an app shows the real system shield for one minute.

Remove every app

Change apps lets you update your selection. Clearing every checkbox saves an empty list automatically. An app on a mandatory break cannot be removed until that break ends.

Where’s the comic?

On supported iOS versions, tap the reminder button to meet the duck. On older versions the button closes the blocked app instead.
